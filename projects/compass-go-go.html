Compass Go Go

A four-player strategy board game with a rotating board, about how teenagers cooperate and compete.

Type
Group project, Pratt Institute
My part
Gameplay design, 3D modeling and visual elements
Timeline

Brief

Our team researched how teenagers balance cooperation and competition, then turned that into a game: a board that rotates as players act, plus resources, event cards, dice and win conditions.

My part

I worked on gameplay design, 3D modeling and visual elements. The research, concept and rules were team work.
