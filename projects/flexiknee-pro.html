Prototype · Solo · Pratt Institute · Apr–Jun 2024

FlexiKnee Pro

A smart knee brace concept for construction workers, who kneel and bend all day, developed from research into a working flex-sensor prototype.

Type
Personal project, Pratt Institute
Role
Solo: research, prototyping, product and interface design
Timeline
April–June 2024
Methods
Market and user research, system map, sensor prototyping, service blueprint
Tools
Flex sensor, NeoPixel / RGB LEDs, silicone casting, 3D modeling and printing

Brief

Construction work means a lot of repeated kneeling and bending. The project explored how a brace could support the knee and make bending visible to the person wearing it.

Research and process

  • Researched existing knee-support products and the workers' needs, then mapped the concept as a system.
  • 3D-printed molds and cast silicone parts while building a flex-sensor prototype.
  • Tested the prototype in simulated postures, such as standing, bending and kneeling, and recorded sensor readings with photos to see how the values changed.
  • Started with a servo that moved to show the bend, then switched to an RGB LED strip because it was easier to wear.

What I made

A brace structure with the sensor and light strip built in, app and watch interface concepts, and a service blueprint that covers setup, daily use, medical consultation and maintenance.

Outcome

A working sensor-and-LED prototype and a full product concept. It wasn't tested on a job site or clinically, so it makes no claims about preventing injuries.
