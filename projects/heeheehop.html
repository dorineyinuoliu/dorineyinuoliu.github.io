Prototype · Solo · Pratt Institute · Sep 2022

HeeHeeHop

A playful shoe accessory meant to get kids moving outdoors: a frog whose tongue-like ribbon shoots out as they run and jump.

Type
Personal project, Pratt Institute
Role
Solo
Timeline
September 2022
Made
Early prototypes, engineering drawings, final white model

Brief and process

I started with a quick experiment, putting a party blower into a squeaky toy to get sound and movement, then refined it into a frog with a ribbon that extends as kids run and jump. Early physical models shaped the mechanism, which I then documented in engineering drawings and a final white model. There's no market launch or measured change in kids' behavior behind it.
