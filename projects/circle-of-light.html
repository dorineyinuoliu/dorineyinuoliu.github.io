Concept · Solo · Pratt Institute · Mar 2023

Circle of Light

An interactive light installation for an imagined New York Aquarium 200 years from now, when plastic waste has replaced sea life.

Type
Personal project, Pratt Institute
Role
Solo
Timeline
March 2023
Recognition
Honorable Mention, IESNYC Student Lighting Competition (2023)

Brief

The installation imagines an aquarium tank two centuries from now. With no sea life left, jellyfish shapes are made from floating plastic waste. When visitors turn the handle, the jellyfish slowly disappear into the garbage.

What I made

A lit jellyfish model and a timeline built into the light. As visitors push it, an arrow moves from 2023 toward the year 3023, through three imagined eras: waste accumulation, decomposition and renewal.

Outcome

A concept with a physical model; it hasn't been built at an aquarium. The project received an Honorable Mention in the IESNYC Student Lighting Competition.
