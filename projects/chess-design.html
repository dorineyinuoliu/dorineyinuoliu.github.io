Chess Design

Two chess-set concepts that trade off differently: one for looks and an easy grip, one for simple assembly and lower cost.

Type
Personal project, Pratt Institute
Role
Solo
Timeline
September 2022
Made
Two piece designs, technical views and renderings

Brief

A redesign of the chess set in two versions. The first focuses on form and pieces that are easy to pick up. The second simplifies the pieces so they're easier to assemble and cheaper to make. The comparison is a design exercise, not a costed or user-tested study.
