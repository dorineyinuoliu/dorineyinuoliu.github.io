Concept + AR prototype · Solo · Pratt Institute · Jan–Mar 2024

Creature Craft

A companion toy for people dealing with loneliness: a modular physical pet with an AR version on iPad that responds to hand gestures.

Type
Personal project, Pratt Institute
Role
Solo
Timeline
January–March 2024
Methods
Market typology, interviews, storyboards
Tools
Unity, Leap Motion, Blender, iPad AR

Brief

Loneliness can come from isolation, a lack of close relationships or just a busy life. The project asked whether a toy that mixes physical play with a digital companion could offer some comfort.

Research and process

  • Sorted existing companion toys on a realistic–fantasy and geometric–rounded map to find an open space.
  • Interviewed potential users about what they'd want from a companion.
  • Designed a toy with magnetic, swappable parts so people can build their own pet.
  • Set up three finger gestures with Leap Motion and wrote the Unity logic that plays the matching pet animation. Modeled the characters in Blender.
  • Storyboarded the experience and designed the iPad interface, with a companion mode and an entertainment mode.

Outcome

A gesture-controlled AR prototype and a physical toy concept. I didn't measure any effect on loneliness.
